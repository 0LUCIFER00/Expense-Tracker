Naming an account (hdfc, paytm, cash) takes the money from it. Leave it out and the expense is only recorded. If two accounts share a word, you are asked which one rather than guessed at. A message starting with + is money coming in, and that one always needs an account. Paid for a group? Splitting is just below.

Splitting a bill over WhatsApp

You pay the whole bill, the others pay you back. Only your share counts as your spending, and what they hand back is a repayment, not income.

split 4 counts you, and can go anywhere in the message — split by 4 and split 4 ways work too. share goes with a + and the account; a word from the expense's name picks which split, and with only one waiting no name is needed. Sending more than is still owed is refused, so the same repayment cannot be recorded twice. On the website it is the Split bill box on Add Expense and Split repayment on Add Income.
